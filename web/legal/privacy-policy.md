## 3. The information we collect

We try to collect as little as possible. Here is everything we handle, grouped by
how it reaches us.

### 3.1 Information you give us

- **Waitlist email address.** If you join the waitlist on the Website, we collect
  the email address you enter and a record of whether you confirmed it.
- **Account information.** When the App launches with accounts, you sign in with
  **Google** or **Facebook**. We receive from that provider your name, email
  address, an account identifier, and (if provided) your profile image. We do not
  receive or store your Google or Facebook password.
- **Project content.** When you use the App, you create "projects" — for example
  a photo of a room, a title, a description, an AI‑generated plan, and the steps
  in it.
- **Photos.** You may take or choose photos of the thing you want to change (a
  "before" photo), and later a "now" photo to show what changed.
- **Notes.** For projects you cannot photograph, you may instead type a short note
  describing what changed. **Please do not paste the underlying work itself** (for
  example, the text of a dissertation, a legal filing, or a private message) —
  Inchmeal only needs a sentence about what moved, not the work.

### 3.2 Information created when you use the Service

- **Server logs.** Like almost all websites and apps, our hosting provider
  automatically records basic technical data when your device connects to our
  servers — such as IP address, device and browser type, and timestamps. We use
  this to keep the Service running, secure, and debuggable.
- **App diagnostics.** The App and the app stores may collect crash and
  performance data to help us fix problems.

We do **not** currently use advertising trackers, and we do not build advertising
profiles about you. If we add product analytics in the future, we will update this
policy first.

### 3.3 Information stored on your device (not on our servers)

Your projects, steps, photos, notes, and progress are stored **locally on your
device** by the App. That content stays on your device unless it is sent for the
limited AI processing described in Section 5. We do not keep a copy of your
projects, photos, or notes on our own servers.

## 5. How your photos and notes are processed by AI

This is the most important section, so we've kept it plain.

To turn your input into a plan and to check your progress, the App sends the
relevant photo(s) and/or note to **Google** and its **Gemini** AI models, which
process them and return a result (a plan, a generated image, or a progress
verdict). This is what makes the core features work.

- We send Google only what is needed for the request — for example the photo you
  captured and a short summary of the project's steps.
- The processing is **transient**: our servers pass your input to Google, return
  the result to your device, and do not store the photo or note afterward.
- Google's handling of data sent to its AI APIs is governed by **Google's terms
  and privacy commitments for its AI/Gemini APIs**. In general, Google states that
  data submitted through its paid Generative AI APIs is not used to train its
  models; however, Google's terms control, and you should review them:
  <https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms> and
  <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

**Two things to keep in mind:**

1. **AI output can be wrong.** Plans, generated images, and progress checks are
   automated best‑guesses and may be inaccurate. Use your own judgement.
2. **Think before you photograph others.** A photo of a room may include people,
   faces, screens, documents, or personal belongings. Only capture what you have
   the right to capture, and avoid including other people (especially children)
   without their consent.

## 9. How long we keep information

- **Waitlist email:** until you ask us to delete it or a reasonable period after
  launch, whichever comes first. Unconfirmed signups may be deleted sooner.
- **Photos and notes sent for AI processing:** not retained by us after the
  request is answered (see Section 5). Google's retention is governed by Google's
  API terms.
- **On‑device project content:** kept on your device until you delete it or remove
  the App. Clearing the App's data or uninstalling it removes this content from
  your device.
- **Account information:** kept while your account is active and deleted within a
  reasonable period after you close it or ask us to.
- **Server logs:** kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

## 11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights over
your personal information:

- **Access** a copy of the information we hold about you.
- **Correct** information that is inaccurate.
- **Delete** your information ("right to be forgotten").
- **Object to** or **restrict** certain processing.
- **Withdraw consent** where we rely on it (for example, waitlist emails).

To exercise any of these, email **hello@molehills.app**. We will respond within
the time required by applicable law. Because much of your content is stored only on

deleting projects in the App, or removing the App.

**EEA/UK:** You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection
authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

**California:** If you are a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA/
CPRA, including the right to know, delete, and correct, and the right to opt out of
"sale" or "sharing" of personal information. **We do not sell or share your
personal information** as those terms are defined by California law, and we do not
process it for cross‑context behavioural advertising. You may exercise your rights
using the contact above; we will not discriminate against you for doing so.
